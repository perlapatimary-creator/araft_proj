Our Introduction

Inspiring the Next Generation of Creators

Aakriti Fashion Design Institute focuses on introducing learners to the art of fashion design, garment construction and embroidery.

We believe that creativity grows through practice, patience and the willingness to learn new skills. Our aim is to help learners explore their interests and strengthen their foundation in fashion.
